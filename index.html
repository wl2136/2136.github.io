About the Institute

Renewable Product AND TCSC-AI is an independent, non-profit research organization committed to advancing knowledge and innovation at the intersection of renewable products and artificial intelligence, with a specific emphasis on TCSC-AI technologies. TCSC-AI was created in 2023.

Our institute operates with a mission to bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical applications.
